Jamie Marsella, PhD

Jamie Marsella is an Assistant Professor in the Department for the Study of Religion at the University of Toronto. She is a historian focusing on religion, gender, and medicine in the North America.

Her research has been supported by fellowships from various institutions, including the New York Academy of Medicine, the American Philosophical Society, the Charles Warren Center for American Studies, the Committee on the Study of Religion at Harvard University, and the Consortium for the History of Science, Technology and Medicine.

Jamie has published writing for academic and public audiences, including in the Bulletin of the History of Medicine, The New England Journal of Medicine, Signs, Journal of the Gilded Age and Progressive Era, Teen Vogue, The Hill, and Nursing Clio.
